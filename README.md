# Employee Attrition Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

**Employee Attrition Analysis** is a data analytics project focused on
understanding the key factors that influence employees to leave an
organization.

The project analyzes employee attrition patterns using **Microsoft
Excel** and **Power BI**, with particular focus on factors such as
**salary, job satisfaction, and work-life balance**.

The analysis is intended to help organizations identify attrition
patterns and make better data-driven decisions to improve employee
retention.

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of the project are to:

-   Analyze employee attrition and identify factors affecting employee
    turnover.
-   Study the impact of salary on employee attrition.
-   Analyze the relationship between job satisfaction and attrition.
-   Examine work-life balance as a factor influencing employee turnover.
-   Identify patterns in employee attrition using data visualization.
-   Support better organizational decisions aimed at improving employee
    retention.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

-   **Microsoft Excel**
-   **Power BI**

## 📈 Analysis Performed

The employee dataset was analyzed using Excel and Power BI. Charts and
dashboards were created to visualize trends and patterns in employee
attrition.

The key areas of analysis include:

### Attrition Rate

Calculation and analysis of the overall employee attrition rate to
understand the extent of employee turnover.

### Department-wise Attrition

Analysis of attrition across different departments to identify areas
with comparatively higher employee turnover.

### Salary vs. Attrition

Analysis of the relationship between employee income/salary and
attrition.

### Job Satisfaction Impact

Analysis of how job satisfaction relates to an employee's likelihood of
leaving the organization.

### Work-Life Balance

The project also considers work-life balance as an important factor
associated with employee attrition.

## 🔍 Key Findings

The analysis identifies several factors associated with higher employee
attrition:

-   **Low job satisfaction** is associated with higher attrition.
-   **Low income** contributes to higher employee attrition.
-   **Long distance from home** is associated with higher attrition.
-   Multiple factors can influence an employee's decision to leave an
    organization.

These findings highlight areas where organizations can focus their
employee-retention strategies.

## 💡 Business Insights

The analysis suggests that organizations can potentially reduce employee
turnover by focusing on:

-   Improving employee **job satisfaction**.
-   Reviewing and improving **salary structures**.
-   Supporting better **work-life balance**.
-   Understanding factors associated with employees who live farther
    from the workplace.
-   Using employee data and dashboards to monitor attrition patterns and
    support retention decisions.

## 🏁 Conclusion

Employee attrition is an important organizational challenge because
employee turnover can affect productivity and increase hiring costs.

This project demonstrates how **Excel and Power BI** can be used to
analyze employee data, visualize attrition patterns, and identify
factors associated with employee turnover. The analysis indicates that
job satisfaction, income, distance from home, and work-life balance are
important areas to consider when developing employee-retention
strategies.
